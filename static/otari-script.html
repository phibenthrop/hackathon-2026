Record yourself: screen recording with your own voice, no AI voice, no AI video.

Before you record

  1. Open /live of this address in the browser. Scroll so the "Otari gateway" panel is visible.
  2. Press Cmd+Shift+5, choose "Record Selected Portion" or "Record Entire Screen".
  3. Click Options and choose your microphone (not "None").
  4. Click Record. Speak slowly. About 90 seconds.
  5. Stop with the stop button in the menu bar.
