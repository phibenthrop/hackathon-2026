„Copy“ legt den Text in die Zwischenablage. Klappt das nicht: Text antippen, er wird ganz markiert.

Project name 

ICP Qualifier

Tagline 

Type any company. Usually in 15 to 25 seconds: a yes or no you can check, and a first draft for the channel the plan recommends. Every verdict needs a word-for-word quote; quotes that are not in the source are thrown out, visibly. Nothing is sent.

Description 

Measured today (20 Nov 2026):
- Scoring model comparison on 12 real companies, same prompt, same quote check:
  GLM-5.3-Flash: 100% valid JSON, 3% of 36 quotes thrown out, 3.3 s, $0.00039 per company.
  gpt-oss-120b: 100% valid JSON, 29% of 35 quotes thrown out, 1.8 s, $0.00057 per company.
  Nemotron-3_5-Lightning: 0% valid JSON.
- A full live run (research, score, plan, draft): usually 15 to 25 seconds; the slowest run we measured took 46; 6 to 9 cents with web research.
  Recorded example: dachser.de, tier A in 23 s for $0.09, 0 of 6 quotes thrown out, German call opener.
- Lead list of 40 real UK cold-chain companies (scored earlier with gpt-oss-120b at default reasoning):
  121 quotes checked, 4 thrown out.

ICP Qualifier is an agent for one decision in outbound: is this company worth contacting, and what do I say first?

You describe your product and your ideal customer profile (or pick one of three presets), then type any company. Four visible steps:
1. Research: Tavily searches the live web and reads the company's site. Every source keeps its URL, which is what makes a quote checkable.
2. Score: GLM-5.3-Flash judges each criterion and must hand over a word-for-word quote. It is small and cheap, and code checks it anyway.
3. Plan: Kimi-K3 picks the role to address, one hook, the channel and what not to say. For German companies it recommends a phone call with a specific reason or a letter, because cold email and LinkedIn messages need prior consent there.
4. Draft: GLM-5.3 writes a short first draft for the channel the plan chose (email, letter text or call opener) in the customer's language.

All model calls go through Nebius Token Factory. Each step shows its model, seconds, tokens and cost, and each step gets a second try or a fallback model if the first answer is unusable.

What is different: the agent may only claim what it can quote. Code checks every quote against the fetched text. A quote that is not there is discarded and shown as discarded, and "Try to fool the gate" lets you type your own sentence and watch the same check answer. The tier A/B/C is computed by code, not by the model. When a company does not fit, the agent stops before the draft and says why; "not enough evidence" is shown apart from "do not contact".

The agent drafts, a person decides. The app cannot send.

Problem / who is it for 

SDRs and founders doing outbound. Today they open ten tabs per company, skim, guess the fit and write from memory. Lead lists are full of look-alikes that are not buyers, and AI research sounds confident but cannot be checked: in our test, one open model had 29% of its proposed quotes thrown out because they were not in the source.

What gets better 

One input instead of ten tabs, and usually 15 to 25 seconds instead of a research session. Every reason for the verdict can be checked with one click before anyone writes to the company. Companies that do not fit are stopped before the draft: the agent says "do not contact" and shows why, or "not enough evidence" and names what to check.

Limits 

Built entirely on 20 Nov 2026 at the event; nothing existed before. The app sends nothing: no sending, no CRM write-back, no contact finding. The lead-list page uses a stored snapshot of 40 UK companies, not a live feed. The filter that drops sentences naming people is a heuristic, not a guarantee. The quote check proves a sentence is in the source, not that the source is right; plan and draft are only checked for valid source references. Company size is rarely on the web, so many companies land on B or "not enough evidence". With the UK cold-chain profile most other companies correctly land on tier C; change the profile to test your own case. Three profile presets are one click away. Consent for cold email is flagged, not checked. Not legal advice.

4. 60-second pitch

Every sales team has the same Monday: a list of two hundred companies, ten tabs per company, a guess, and an email from memory.
This is ICP Qualifier. I type one company. Tavily researches it on the live web. Then three open models on Nebius Token Factory do three jobs. GLM 5.3 Flash scores, because it is cheap and our code checks it anyway. Kimi K3 plans, because that step needs judgement. GLM 5.3 writes the draft.
The rule is simple: no quote, no claim. Every verdict needs a word-for-word quote from the source. Code checks it. If it is not there, it is thrown out, and you can try to fool the check yourself.
We measured it on twelve real companies. GLM Flash: three percent of quotes thrown out. GPT OSS 120b: twenty-nine percent. So we took GLM Flash.
One company usually takes fifteen to twenty-five seconds and a few cents. The agent drafts. A person decides. Nothing is sent.
Now give me a company, with its domain.
