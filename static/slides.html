Problem

Lookalike lists are easy. Deciding who is worth an email is not.

  • A similar-companies search returns dozens of names in seconds.
  • Someone still opens every website to check fit: industry, size, region, buying signals.
  • A generic AI "fit score" without a source is a guess; one wrong claim in a first email burns the lead.

Solution

ICP Qualifier: every verdict comes with a quote.

  • Derives your ICP from the websites of your three best customers, as editable criteria.
  • Reads each candidate's website and judges every criterion.
  • Tier A/B/C with a verbatim quote and a link to the passage. No quote, no verdict: "no evidence" lowers the tier.

Agent drafts, human sends.

How it works

  1. Best customers in - ICP derived: industry terms, size range, region, signals.
  2. Similar companies from a company source; existing customers removed automatically.
  3. Agent qualifies each website; every quote is checked character by character against the source text.
  4. Review and export - sort, filter, expand the evidence, export CSV for the campaign tool or CRM.

Model behind an OpenAI-compatible interface; without a key a deterministic rule scorer runs the same pipeline.

Where it sits

similarcompanies.aiReturns companies similar to your customers. "Qualification, people and CRM come from your agent's other tools."
→
ICP QualifierQualifies each company against your ICP, with evidence. Removes existing customers. Tiers A/B/C.
→
Needle / CRMRuns the outbound campaign from the reviewed lead list. A human decides what is sent.

Real data path in this demo: three seed companies → find_companies on the similarcompanies.ai MCP server (paged only by search id) → seed brands and duplicate domains removed → public company websites fetched (robots.txt respected, no contact, team or legal pages, no personal data kept) → every verdict passes the verbatim-quote check and links to the real page. Pulled once on our box and stored as a snapshot, so the public demo needs no token.
