Problem

Lookalike lists are easy. Deciding who is worth an email is not.

  • A similar-companies search returns dozens of names in seconds.
  • Someone still opens every website to check fit: industry, size, region, buying signals.
  • A generic AI "fit score" without a source is a guess; one wrong claim in a first email burns the lead.

Solution

ICP Qualifier: every verdict comes with a quote.

  • Derives your ICP from the websites of your three best customers, as editable criteria.
  • Reads each candidate's website and judges every criterion.
  • Tier A/B/C with a verbatim quote and a link to the passage. No quote, no verdict: "no evidence" lowers the tier.

Agent drafts, human sends.

How it works

  1. Best customers in - ICP derived: industry terms, size range, region, signals.
  2. Similar companies from a company source; existing customers removed automatically.
  3. Agent qualifies each website; every quote is checked character by character against the source text.
  4. Review and export - sort, filter, expand the evidence, export CSV for the campaign tool or CRM.

Model behind an OpenAI-compatible interface (open models on Nebius Token Factory); it also says whether a company is an operator at all or a publisher, manufacturer or spam page, with a verbatim quote. Without a key the app replays precomputed verdicts or runs a deterministic rule scorer.

Pulled once on our box and stored as a snapshot, so the public demo needs no token.

Limits

  • Built entirely today, 20 Nov 2026. Nothing existed before the event.
  • Works: live research, quote-checked scoring, plan, draft, for any company with a public website.
  • Not built: sending, CRM write-back, contact finding. Sending is left out on purpose.
  • Weak spots: company size is rarely on the web; the quote check proves a sentence exists, not that it is true; German cold email needs consent, the app only warns.
  • The lead-list page runs on a stored snapshot of 40 UK companies, not a live feed.

Next steps

  • Batch mode: score 50 companies with the small model, plan and write only for tier A.
  • Get company size from a source that has it; today the real dataset has no size data, so size is not scored.
  • Hand A-tier leads to a Needle campaign as drafts for human review.
  • Learn from won and lost deals to adjust criteria weights.
