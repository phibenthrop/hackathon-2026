Brennholt Cold Chain Services GmbH provides temperature-controlled storage and distribution for food and pharmaceutical customers in northern Bavaria and Franconia, Germany. Our motto: the right temperature, every time.

[page: /about]
Brennholt was founded in Nuremberg in 1985. Today the company has 400 employees at three sites and operates a fleet of 110 refrigerated trucks. Our cold storage capacity totals 30,000 pallet spaces in the chilled, frozen and controlled ambient ranges.

[page: /quality]
We hold IFS Logistics certification for food and are GDP certified for pharmaceuticals. Our quality department supports more than thirty customer audits per year. Temperature records from the warehouses are digital, while the records from trucks are still collected on paper and scanned.

[page: /news]
We are building a new warehouse in Bamberg for pharmaceutical customers. The expansion adds 8,000 temperature-controlled pallet spaces and is scheduled to open in two years.
