Kiesgrund Bau GmbH is a construction company in Lower Austria. We build residential houses, small apartment buildings and agricultural halls, and we carry out renovations and extensions.

[page: /about]
Kiesgrund was founded in 1983 in the Waldviertel region of Austria. Today 110 employees work for us, including bricklayers, carpenters, site managers and an in-house planning office. We cover projects within about 80 kilometres of our yard in Zwettl.

[page: /services]
Our services include shell construction, complete turnkey houses, timber frame extensions, roof renovations and the energy-efficient refurbishment of older buildings. Customers get a fixed price and a fixed handover date before construction begins. Our planning office also handles building permits with the local authorities.

[page: /references]
In recent years we have completed more than 200 single-family houses, three community centres and several large stables for dairy farms in the region. References and photos are available on request.
