Haldenfeld Obst Genossenschaft is a fruit growers' cooperative in the canton of Thurgau, Switzerland. Our members grow apples, pears and cherries, and we take care of everything after the harvest: sorting, storage, packing and sale.

[page: /about]
The cooperative was founded in 1952 by eleven farming families. Today it has 140 member farms and employs 85 people at our packing station. Most of our fruit is sold to Swiss retail chains under their own brands, a smaller part goes to local juice producers.

[page: /storage]
After the harvest, the apples are stored in our controlled atmosphere cold storage, which keeps them fresh until the following summer. The 42 storage rooms hold 9,000 tonnes of fruit. Low oxygen and a constant temperature of around one degree slow down ripening. Our storage manager checks the rooms every day and adjusts the settings for each variety.

[page: /news]
This year's harvest was slightly below average because of a late frost in April, but the quality of the fruit is excellent. The cherry season starts again in June.
