Hallerau Frischelogistik GmbH delivers fresh produce, herbs and dairy products to supermarkets and restaurants in Munich and southern Bavaria, Germany. Fresh food is our only business, and temperature-controlled logistics is the only way we do it.

[page: /about]
Our company was founded in 1991 at the Munich wholesale market. Today 210 employees run our cross-dock and cold storage in Garching and a fleet of 70 refrigerated trucks. We receive goods from growers in Italy, Spain and the Netherlands every night and deliver them before the shops open.

[page: /quality]
We are IFS Logistics certified. Customers and inspectors expect clear proof that the cold chain was never broken, so every audit starts with our temperature records. Right now these records come from paper checklists on each truck and a manual export from our warehouse system. Our quality manager spends many hours each month combining them.

[page: /news]
Hallerau is expanding to Augsburg. A new site with 3,000 square metres of chilled space will open next spring and serve Swabia and the Allgaeu region.
