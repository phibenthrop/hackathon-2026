Tintenfeld Creative Studio Lda. is a branding and design studio in Lisbon, Portugal. We create brand identities, websites and campaigns for start-ups, hotels and cultural institutions.

[page: /about]
The studio was founded in 2017 by two designers who met at art school. Today our team of 18 people includes designers, copywriters, developers and a photographer. We work in Portuguese, English and Spanish, and we have clients in Portugal, Spain and the United Kingdom.

[page: /work]
Recent work includes the rebranding of a boutique hotel group in the Algarve, a website for a contemporary dance festival and packaging design for a craft gin distillery. Each project starts with a workshop in which we try to understand what makes the client different.

[page: /approach]
We believe that good design is simple, honest and memorable. Instead of large teams and long presentations, we prefer small teams and quick prototypes that clients can react to.
