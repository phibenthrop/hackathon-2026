Ambrosin Textil Sp. z o.o. is a textile wholesaler in Lodz, Poland. We supply fashion brands, uniform makers and upholstery workshops with woven fabrics, knitwear and linings.

[page: /about]
Ambrosin was founded in 2001 in the heart of Poland's traditional textile region. Our team of 55 people manages a stock of more than 4,000 fabrics in our warehouse. We buy directly from weaving mills in Poland, Italy and Turkey and sell to customers across Central and Eastern Europe.

[page: /services]
Customers can order from one metre for samples up to full rolls for production. Fabrics are cut to length in our warehouse and shipped within 48 hours by parcel or pallet. Our colour matching service helps designers find the right shade for their collection.

[page: /news]
Our autumn fabric fair takes place in Lodz in September. Designers and buyers are welcome to see the new wool and linen qualities and to talk to our team about custom dyeing.
