Quellbeck Kuehlhaus GmbH is a cold storage and distribution partner for food producers in the Ruhr area of Germany. We store chilled and frozen goods in our warehouse in Dortmund and deliver them with our own refrigerated trucks to retail and food service customers.

[page: /about]
Quellbeck started in 1998 as a small cold store for local butchers. Today we have 140 employees and handle more than 1,200 pallets per day. Our customers range from bakeries with frozen dough to importers of fresh fish. Keeping the cold chain closed is what we are paid for, and we take it personally.

[page: /quality]
Our sites follow HACCP and are audited by retail customers twice a year. Before each audit, the team collects temperature printouts from every cold room and files them by hand, which takes several days. We know this has to become simpler.

[page: /news]
We are expanding into a new site in Bochum with 5,000 additional pallet spaces for frozen goods. Opening is planned for the second half of next year.
