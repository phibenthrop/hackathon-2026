Brumtaler Frischedienst GmbH supplies restaurants, canteens and retailers in northwestern Switzerland with fresh produce, dairy and meat. Our temperature-controlled logistics keep salads crisp and yoghurt safe, from the farm to the kitchen door.

[page: /about]
The company was founded near Basel in 1987 by three vegetable growers. Today 240 staff work at our distribution centre, in purchasing and on the road. We operate 8,500 square metres of cold storage with separate zones for fruit, vegetables, dairy and meat, and a fleet of refrigerated trucks with multi-temperature compartments.

[page: /quality]
Food safety is the core of our business. Our distribution centre is IFS Logistics certified and passes an unannounced audit every year. We follow HACCP principles in every step, from goods receipt to delivery. Temperatures in the cold rooms and trucks are checked several times per shift and recorded in spreadsheets, which our quality team reviews every morning. Any deviation in the cold chain is documented and reported to the customer.
