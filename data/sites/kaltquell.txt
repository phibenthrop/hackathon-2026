Kaltquell Logistik GmbH keeps food cold from the producer to the supermarket shelf. We run cold storage and refrigerated transport for regional dairies, butchers and fresh food wholesalers across northern Germany. Our cold chain covers chilled goods between 2 and 8 degrees and frozen goods down to minus 25 degrees.

[page: /about]
Kaltquell was founded in Hamburg in 1994 as a family business with three trucks. Today we are a team of 180 employees operating two cold storage sites and a fleet of 64 refrigerated trucks. Our customers expect every pallet to arrive at the right temperature, and so do the food inspectors. Every site passes regular HACCP audits, and our quality team prepares for each audit weeks in advance. Temperature readings from the loading docks are still written into paper logs by the shift leads and scanned at the end of each week.

[page: /news]
Construction of our new warehouse in Neumuenster starts in spring. The site will add 9,000 pallet spaces of cold storage and connect directly to the motorway towards Denmark. With the additional capacity we want to serve more producers from Schleswig-Holstein.
