Lumbra Fresh Foods B.V. imports, ripens and distributes fruit and vegetables from all over the world. From our facility in the port of Rotterdam, Netherlands, we supply supermarkets and wholesalers in the Netherlands and Belgium.

[page: /about]
Lumbra was founded in 2004 by two traders from the Rotterdam fruit market. Today we have 150 employees. Our facility has 18 ripening rooms for bananas and avocados and 11,000 square metres of cold storage for berries, leafy greens and exotic fruit. Fruit leaves our dock in refrigerated trucks within hours of being ordered.

[page: /quality]
We are certified according to IFS Logistics and BRCGS, and our customers audit us at least once a year. Quality controllers check every incoming container. Temperatures in the ripening rooms are logged manually twice per shift.

[page: /news]
We are expanding our ripening capacity with six new rooms. The extension will be ready by next summer and allows us to supply additional retail customers in Flanders.
