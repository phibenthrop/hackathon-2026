Tiefgrund Frozen Logistics GmbH stores and delivers frozen food for manufacturers, importers and food service companies. Our deep-freeze warehouse in Bremen, Germany, holds 22,000 pallet spaces at minus 24 degrees, directly next to the port.

[page: /services]
We offer cold storage, order picking, blast freezing and refrigerated distribution to retail warehouses across northern and western Germany. Imported goods are cleared, checked and stored within hours of arriving in the port, so the cold chain is never interrupted. Our quality management is IFS Logistics certified, and our temperature logs are available for every audit.

[page: /about]
Tiefgrund was founded in 1979 by a fishing cooperative. Today 210 employees work in the warehouse, in the office and on the road. Many of them have been with us for more than twenty years.

[page: /news]
We are building additional capacity: a new warehouse wing with 6,000 pallet spaces will be ready next autumn. At the same time we are hiring forklift drivers and shift supervisors for the night shift.
