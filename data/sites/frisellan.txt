Frisellan Cold Chain AG moves pharmaceutical products that must never get too warm. From our hub in Linz, Austria, we deliver vaccines, insulin and clinical trial samples to hospitals, pharmacies and laboratories in Austria, Bavaria and the Czech Republic.

[page: /services]
Our temperature-controlled services cover three ranges: frozen below minus 20 degrees, refrigerated between 2 and 8 degrees, and controlled ambient between 15 and 25 degrees. Every shipment travels in validated boxes or in one of our refrigerated vans. We work according to the EU guidelines on Good Distribution Practice (GDP) and are GDP certified since 2016. Customers receive a temperature record for each delivery on request.

[page: /about]
Frisellan started in 2009 with four couriers. Today we are a team of 95 people, including pharmacists, dispatchers and drivers. Our cold chain is audited by pharmaceutical manufacturers several times a year, and we treat every audit as a chance to improve.
