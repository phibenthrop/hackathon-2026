Zandoria Backzutaten GmbH supplies bakeries and pastry shops in Austria with flour, baking mixes, seeds, nuts and decorations. Whatever a bakery needs on its shelf, we deliver it the next working day.

[page: /about]
Zandoria was founded in Vienna in 1999 by a baker and a miller. Today we have 80 employees in Vienna and in our second warehouse near Linz. We carry more than 3,500 products from 120 suppliers, from organic rye flour to chocolate decorations.

[page: /products]
All our products are dry goods that are stored at room temperature. Some customers ask about frozen dough pieces; we do not carry frozen products, but we are happy to recommend partners. Our bakery advisors visit customers regularly and help with new recipes and seasonal products.
