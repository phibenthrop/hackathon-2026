Velmora Kuehltransporte GmbH is a refrigerated transport company based in Leipzig, Germany. We move chilled and frozen food for producers and wholesalers between Saxony, Thuringia and Berlin, and we take care of the cold chain from the first pick-up to the last delivery.

[page: /about]
Velmora was founded in 2002. With 130 employees and 48 refrigerated trucks, we are one of the larger independent carriers in central Germany. Our own cold storage hall in Leipzig-North holds 4,000 pallets at minus 22 degrees. All drivers are trained in HACCP basics, and our sites are audited by major food retailers.

[page: /news]
This year we added twelve new trucks with dual-temperature bodies. Next we are expanding our cross-dock in Erfurt so that customers in Thuringia get same-day delivery.
