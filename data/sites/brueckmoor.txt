Brueckmoor Moebelwerkstatt GmbH builds solid wood furniture to order in Upper Bavaria, Germany. Tables, cabinets, beds and complete kitchens, designed with our customers and built in our own workshop.

[page: /about]
Our workshop was founded in 1968 by a master carpenter and his wife. Today 22 carpenters, designers and fitters work for us. We use oak, walnut, ash and cherry from sustainably managed forests in the Alpine region.

[page: /craft]
Every piece is planned digitally, but the joints, surfaces and finishes are still done by hand. We oil our furniture with natural oils and avoid synthetic lacquers. A dining table takes about six weeks from order to delivery, a fitted kitchen about three months.

[page: /showroom]
Visit our showroom next to the workshop and feel the wood yourself. Our designers take time to understand your room and your daily routines before they draw the first sketch.
