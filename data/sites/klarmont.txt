Klarmont Catering Logistik GmbH delivers ready meals, salads and desserts from central kitchens to schools, hospitals and company canteens in Cologne and Bonn, Germany. Every meal arrives chilled and on time.

[page: /services]
Our refrigerated vans pick up food from the central kitchens in the early morning and deliver to more than 300 locations before 11:00. We also collect reusable containers and return them to the kitchens for cleaning. On request we offer temperature-controlled storage for catering companies that need extra space during peak season.

[page: /about]
Klarmont was established in 2013 as a spin-off of a regional catering group. Since then we have built a network of kitchens, drivers and depots that covers the whole Cologne-Bonn region. We see ourselves as the invisible part of every good school lunch.
