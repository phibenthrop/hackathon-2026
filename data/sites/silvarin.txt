Silvarin Pharma Logistics GmbH stores and distributes medicines for pharmaceutical manufacturers and wholesalers. From our temperature-controlled warehouse near Frankfurt, Germany, we supply pharmacies, hospitals and clinics throughout Germany and Austria.

[page: /services]
Our services include temperature-controlled storage between 2 and 8 degrees and between 15 and 25 degrees, frozen storage for biologics, pick and pack, and refrigerated delivery in validated vehicles. We act as an extension of our clients' quality systems and keep the cold chain documented from goods receipt to the final signature.

[page: /about]
Silvarin was founded in 2005 and today employs 320 employees across two sites. We are GDP certified and hold a wholesale licence. Each of our clients audits us at least once a year, and our quality team handles more than twenty audits annually.

[page: /news]
We have signed the lease for a new site in Kassel. The warehouse adds 12,000 pallet spaces and will be fully temperature-controlled. With this expansion we can offer next-day delivery to all of central Germany.
